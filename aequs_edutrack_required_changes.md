# Aequs EduTrack --- Required Changes v3

## 1. Branding & UI: AF Logo Integration

-   Add the **Aequs Foundation (AF) Logo** prominently across the entire platform.
-   Include the AF Logo in the main navigation sidebar, top application bar, and user login/authentication screens.
-   Embed the AF Logo on all printable documents, exports, certificates, and student ID cards.
-   Ensure consistent brand color palette (Aequs Red, Slate, and Dark tones) throughout all interface components.

---

## 2. Individual Student Details & Student ID Card Feature

-   Add a dedicated **Individual Student Details** page/profile.
-   Maintain complete student lifecycle information in one centralized view (demographics, enrollment, family/guardian info, contact details).
-   Include academic performance, attendance, and student participation history across all NGO and Foundation programs.
-   Track all benefits, materials, and support received by each individual student.
-   Allow authorized administrators to edit, update, and manage student profiles.
-   **Student ID Card Feature**:
    -   Generate and preview printable/downloadable Student ID cards.
    -   Include student photograph, full name, admission number, school, grade, date of birth, emergency contact, and AF Logo.
    -   Incorporate a scannable QR code on each ID card linked to the student's digital profile for rapid verification.
    -   Support single ID card generation and bulk PDF export for entire classes/schools.

---

## 3. Separate Subject Tracking & Academics

-   Implement **Separate Subject Tracking** across all student academic records.
-   Allow schools and programs to log and monitor performance by individual subject (e.g., Mathematics, Science, English, Regional Language, Social Studies).
-   Track subject-wise marks, grades, attendance, exam cycles (Unit Tests, Midterms, Annuals), and syllabus progress.
-   Provide subject-specific performance analytics to identify areas where students need remedial assistance or advanced mentorship.
-   Support subject-level baseline vs. endline evaluation tracking.

---

## 4. Detailed EV Class Tracking

-   Introduce dedicated and **Detailed EV (Exposure Visit / Experiential Learning / Environmental Education) Class Tracking**.
-   Maintain records for each EV session, including:
    -   Session topic, curriculum module, and learning objectives
    -   Date, time slot, duration, and facilitator/trainer details
    -   Partner school, batch, and grade level involved
    -   Host venue, exposure visit location, or field route
-   Log detailed student attendance and active participation for each EV class.
-   Record learning materials/kits utilized during EV classes.
-   Capture pre-session and post-session assessment scores, student feedback, and photo documentation.

---

## 5. NGO Management

Add separate NGO profiles/modules for partner organizations:

-   **Pratham**
-   **Agastya**
-   **Youth for Seva**

-   Associate students with their respective NGO programs where applicable.

    -   Partner schools and beneficiary communities
    -   Enrolled students and cohorts

## 8. Scholarship Tracking Tab

-   Add a dedicated **Scholarship Tracking Tab** in the navigation portal.
-   Maintain comprehensive records of internal Foundation scholarships and partner-funded educational aids.
-   Key capabilities:
    -   Scholarship application management and verification workflow
    -   Student eligibility checking based on academic merit, attendance, and socio-economic criteria

## 9. Government Scholarship & Employee Special Tab

-   Introduce a dedicated **Government Scholarship & Employee Special Tab** in the portal.

    -   Track applications and status for State and Central government scholarship schemes (e.g., SSP - State Scholarship Portal, NSP - National Scholarship Portal, Pre-Matric/Post-Matric scholarships, minority & category-specific schemes).

    -   Approval workflows and disbursement records for employee-specific welfare initiatives.

## 17. Archive Tab & Data Lifecycle Management

-   Introduce a dedicated **Archive Tab** in the navigation system.
-   Provide a clean separation between active operational data and historical records.
-   Archive capabilities:
    -   Archive completed or inactive projects and programs.
    -   Archive graduated, transferred, or inactive student profiles.
    -   Archive past events, campaigns, and completed volunteer rosters.
    -   Archive closed inventory batches and historical distribution ledgers.
-   Features of the Archive Tab:
    -   Filter by archive category (Projects, Programs, Students, Events, Inventory).
    -   Search and view archived records in read-only mode for audit purposes.
    -   Restore archived items back to active state when necessary.
    -   Role-restricted access (Super Admin / Admin only) to archive or restore records.

---

## 18. Data Protection Act Compliance (DPDP Compliance)

-   Ensure full compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act)** and applicable data privacy standards.
-   **Parental & Guardian Consent**:
    -   Capture and record verified parental/guardian consent before storing minor students' personal information and photos.
    -   Store consent timestamps and consent forms/modes (physical form upload or digital acknowledgment).
-   **PII Protection & Data Masking**:
    -   Mask sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (Aadhaar numbers, bank account details, exact residential addresses) in standard views.
    -   Restrict full PII visibility strictly to authorized administrators with audit log tracking.
-   **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**:
    -   Enforce strict data access boundaries so that volunteers, external coordinators, and staff only access the minimum necessary data.
-   **Data Retention & Right to Erasure**:
    -   Define data retention policies for student records upon graduation or exit.
    -   Provide mechanisms for data rectification, anonymization, and compliant record archiving/deletion.
-   **Audit Trails**:
    -   Maintain immutable security audit logs for all access, export, update, and deletion of sensitive personal data.

The system will be enhanced to provide:

7.  **Government Scholarship & Employee Special Tab**: Dedicated tracking for government scholarship schemes and Aequs employee child education assistance.
8.  **Mandatory Location Field in Every Program**: Standardized location information on all program records for geospatial analysis.
9.  **Event Reminder Feature**: Automated notification and reminder alerts for upcoming events and volunteer activities.
10. **Dedicated Archive Tab**: Unified portal to view, manage, and safely restore inactive or completed records across all modules.
11. **Data Protection Act Compliance**: DPDP-compliant privacy safeguards, parental consent logs, PII masking, RBAC, and access audit logs.
12. **NGO Management**: Dedicated modules and reporting for Pratham, Agastya, and Youth for Seva.
13. **Monthly Tracking**: Multi-dimensional monthly tracking across students, NGOs, projects, and benefits.
14. **CSR & School Integration**: Transparent linking of CSR funding with school infrastructure and educational programs.
15. **Student Benefits & Project Inventory**: End-to-end material and resource distribution tracking.
